NexusNova Android Nova Vault v1 build contract

- Every fresh mining start is gated by the native mining-start interstitial and starts only after dismissal.
- Natural 24-hour mining completion grants exactly one pending Nova Vault.
- Stored Nova Booster / Nova Rain / 24H Time Warp value is server-authoritative.
- Nova Vault open, Booster, Rain and Time Warp share a 15-second cooldown.
- Time Warp completes one session but never grants another Vault.
- Rewarded TEST ads never change mining time or NVX.
