Delete your NexusNova account

This page explains how to request deletion of your NexusNova account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the same email address used for your NexusNova account.
  2. Use the subject NexusNova Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address of the account you want deleted.
  4. We may ask you to verify account ownership before completing deletion.
Request Account Deletion

What will be deleted

After verification, NexusNova will delete or anonymize account-related data that it controls, including data such as:

  • account/profile information associated with the deleted account;
  • cloud-backed NexusNova app state tied to that account;
  • mining, reward, task, referral, and preference data associated with that account, where deletion is legally and technically permitted.

What may be retained

Limited records may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, abuse prevention, or other lawful obligations. Data held independently by third-party providers is handled according to their own retention policies.

Important:

Deleting your NexusNova account is intended to be permanent. Once deletion is completed, account access and recoverable NexusNova account data may no longer be available.
