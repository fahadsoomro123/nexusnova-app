Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NexusNova handles information when you use the NexusNova Android app, its web-based app shell, and related features. NexusNova is a multi-purpose utility app that may include account services, mining/reward points, Nova Hub tools, market/news features, location-based tools, browser utilities, advertising, and other optional modules.

1. Information we may collect or process

The information processed depends on which NexusNova features you choose to use.

  • Account and profile data: such as your email address, display name, account identifier, authentication state, profile information, referral information, and related account settings.
  • App activity and reward data: such as mining-session state, point/reward balances, completed tasks, referral status, feature usage state, and preferences needed to provide NexusNova functions.
  • Location data: coarse or precise location may be accessed when you actively use features such as Nova Drive, Nova Track, location, travel, or other location-based tools. The current NexusNova Android build is designed for foreground location use and does not request Android background-location permission.
  • Camera and microphone: these may be accessed only when you use a feature that needs them, such as scanning, camera-based utilities, voice/audio input, or similar tools, and only after the relevant Android permission is granted.
  • Files and user-selected content: if you choose a file, image, document, or other content through a file picker or feature, NexusNova processes the item you select for the requested function.
  • Caller-ID / phonebook feature data: if you choose to use NexusNova caller-related tools, NexusNova may process phone numbers, labels, names, or address information that you intentionally enter or save for that feature. NexusNova does not require broad Android Contacts access in the current build.
  • Wallet and blockchain information: if wallet features are used, NexusNova may process public wallet addresses, public blockchain balances, network information, and transaction data needed to display those features. Never share a seed phrase or private key with anyone.
  • Device, network and diagnostic information: service providers may process device identifiers, IP address, browser/WebView or device information, network state, ad-performance information, security signals, and error/diagnostic data where needed to operate, protect, or improve their services.

2. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • create and authenticate NexusNova accounts;
  • provide and synchronize app features, balances, mining/reward state, tasks, preferences, and user-requested tools;
  • provide location-based, camera, microphone, file, wallet, browser, and utility features when you choose to use them;
  • protect account integrity, prevent abuse, diagnose failures, and maintain app reliability;
  • serve, measure, limit, and improve advertising in accordance with user consent and applicable law;
  • respond to support, privacy, security, or deletion requests.

3. Google Firebase and related services

NexusNova uses Google/Firebase services for functions such as authentication and cloud-backed app data. Depending on the feature and configuration, information such as account identifiers, email address, app state, reward/mining data, and related records may be stored or processed through Firebase services.

Google's privacy information is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Advertising, Google AdMob and consent

NexusNova uses Google Mobile Ads / AdMob. Google and participating advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions, approximate location derived from network information, consent signals, and ad-performance data to deliver, measure, prevent fraud in, and improve advertising.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, NexusNova uses or intends to use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) / certified consent framework to request required choices before personalized advertising is served. Where required, you can consent, manage choices, or withdraw consent through the available privacy controls.

Some NexusNova features may use rewarded advertising. Rewarded-ad interactions are voluntary where presented as an optional reward mechanism. Ad availability is not guaranteed.

Learn more about Google's advertising and privacy practices at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

5. Cookies, local storage and device storage

The NexusNova web/app shell and third-party services may use cookies, WebView storage, local storage, identifiers, or similar technologies for sign-in state, feature preferences, security, consent choices, service functionality, and advertising. Where legally required, consent choices are requested before non-essential advertising or personalization storage is used.

6. Sharing of information

NexusNova does not sell personal information for money. Information may be shared or processed only as reasonably necessary with:

  • service providers such as Google/Firebase and Google AdMob that operate authentication, storage, security, consent, advertising, and related technical services;
  • external data or API providers when a feature you request needs to contact that provider, for example market, news, travel, map, or other utility data services;
  • legal or security recipients if disclosure is reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect users, investigate abuse, or defend rights and systems.

7. Permissions and user control

Android permissions are requested only when a feature requires them. You can deny or later revoke permissions such as location, camera, or microphone through Android settings. Some related NexusNova features may not work without the permission they require.

8. Data retention

We retain account and cloud-backed app data for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security and integrity, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, or fulfill the purpose for which the data was collected. Local app/WebView data may remain on your device until it is cleared, removed by the app, or the app is uninstalled. Third-party providers retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations.

9. Account and data deletion

You may request deletion of your NexusNova account and associated personal data by contacting the privacy address below. After verifying the request, we will delete or anonymize account-related data that NexusNova controls, except information that must be retained for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or other lawful purposes.

Before NexusNova is submitted to Google Play, the app's account-deletion flow and Play Console disclosures will be reviewed so that the in-app and web deletion options match the published policy and actual data handling.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect personal information, including secure network transport and access controls where supported by the services NexusNova uses. However, no internet, mobile, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Children's privacy

NexusNova is a general-audience utility application and is not designed specifically for children under 13. If local law requires parental or guardian consent for a young user, that consent should be obtained before the user provides personal information or uses personalized advertising features.

12. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection, or withdrawal of consent concerning personal data. You may also manage Android permissions on your device and, where available, reopen advertising privacy choices from NexusNova's privacy/settings controls.

13. Third-party links and services

NexusNova may open websites, browser content, external services, market/news sources, maps, or other third-party resources. Those services operate under their own privacy policies. NexusNova is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites or services that you choose to use.

14. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when NexusNova features, data practices, advertising configuration, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.
